## ✨ Features Showcase

<details open>
<summary><b>🔋 Intelligent Power Delivery</b></summary>
<br>

- **Solar Conversion:** Efficiently harvests solar energy to charge the internal lithium-ion batteries.
- **Regulated Output:** Provides stable 5V USB output for charging mobile devices safely.
- **Overcharge Protection:** Integrated TP4056 ensures batteries are protected from overcharging and deep discharging.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📦 Custom 3D Printed Enclosure</b></summary>
<br>

- **Durable Design:** Fully customized 3D models tailored for perfect component fitting.
- **Portability:** Compact and lightweight chassis designed for outdoor usage and travel.
- **Heat Management:** Strategically placed vents to keep the electronics cool under direct sunlight.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💻 Smart Monitoring (Optional)</b></summary>
<br>

- **Real-time Stats:** Firmware tracks battery voltage and charging status.
- **Status Indicators:** LED lights provide immediate feedback on charging/fully charged states.
- **Extensible Codebase:** Built with standard C/C++ in Arduino IDE for easy modifications and upgrades.

</details>

## 📖 Hardware Specifications - Quick Reference

| Component | Details | Function |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ☀️ **Solar Panel** | 5V / 6V Mini Solar Panel | Captures solar energy |
| 🔋 **Battery** | 18650 Li-ion Battery | Stores harvested energy |
| ⚡ **Charge Controller** | TP4056 Module | Manages battery charging and protection |
| 🔌 **Boost Converter** | 5V Step-up Module | Boosts battery voltage to 5V for USB output |
| 🧠 **MCU (Optional)** | Arduino UNO | Controls smart features and monitors voltage |
| 📦 **Chassis** | Custom 3D Printed PLA/PETG | Houses and protects all electronics |
